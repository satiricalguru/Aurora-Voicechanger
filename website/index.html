06 · Questions

Good to know.

When can I download it?

Aurora Voice is still in development. Downloads will appear on this page when the first release is ready.

Which systems will it support?

macOS on Apple Silicon comes first. Windows is planned.

Will it work in Discord and games?

Yes. Aurora Voice installs its own virtual microphone, Aurora Microphone, which any app can select as its input.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes. Drag MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, AIFF or FLAC files (up to two minutes each) onto the soundboard, then give each one a hotkey, a volume and a loop setting.

Does noise removal add delay?

A little. Low latency mode adds about 5 ms, Balanced 15 ms and Studio 30 ms. Pick the trade-off that suits you.

Does my voice get uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing, including the AI voices, happens on your own computer.
